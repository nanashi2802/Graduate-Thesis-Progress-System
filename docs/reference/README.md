# Reference sources

This folder holds the official PUP Graduate School source files. The Unified Thesis Process (`docs/PUP_CpE_Thesis_Progress_System_Unified_Thesis_Process_v1.2.md`) derives from them. Its source order puts the TDW forms first and the Guidelines second.

These files hold no project rules. Do not edit them. If a new version arrives, add it next to the old one.

## Folders

| Folder | Content |
| :---- | :---- |
| `tdw-forms/` | The 13 blank TDW forms (`.doc`, original files) |
| `tdw-forms/text/` | A plain-text extract of each form (made with `antiword`). Use it to search and cite. |
| `guidelines/` | Guidelines on the Implementation of the Modified Thesis/Dissertation Writing Courses (controlled copy, PDF) |
| `guidelines/text/` | A plain-text extract of the Guidelines (made with `pdftotext -layout`) |
| `AGENTS_md_Reference.md` | Guide for the structure of `AGENTS.md` |

## TDW forms

| Form | Title | Milestone | Key fields |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| TDW 01 | Concept Paper Adviser Endorsement Form | Stage 0 (not tracked) | Faculty, candidate, program, tentative title. Dean/Director signs. Faculty accepts. |
| TDW 02 | Thesis/Dissertation Advising Contract | Stage 0 (not tracked) | Adviser and Advisee duties. No-fault termination through the Dean. Program Chair and GS Secretary witness. |
| TDW 03 | Consultation and Monitoring Form | 1, 2, 3 | Phase (Proposal, Pre-Final, Final). Contact details. Rows: date and time, place or platform, topics, Adviser signature. |
| TDW 04 | Adviser Appointment and Acknowledgement Form | Stage 0 (not tracked) | Title, proponent, program. Adviser certifies the advisee load is within the limit. |
| TDW 05 | Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement | 1 and 3 | Examiner, University representative, thesis author and title. Return or delete copies within 10 days after the approved revised copy. |
| TDW 06 | Committee Appointment and Acceptance Form | 1 | Start term, comprehensive exam date. Adviser plus 2 (thesis) or 3 (dissertation) evaluators accept. Program Chair recommends. Dean/Director approves. |
| TDW 07 | Proposal Defense Endorsement Form | 1 | Schedule of defense. Committee signs. Attachments: proposal manuscript, ethics certification. Program Chair notes. Dean/Director approves. |
| TDW 08 | Proposal Defense Evaluation Sheet | 1 | Rubric: manuscript 60% (10, 20, 20, 10), oral defense 40% (10, 10, 20). Comments. Grade equivalent table. Action Taken. |
| TDW 09 | Pre-Final Endorsement Form | 2 | Schedule of defense. Committee signs. Attachments: pre-final manuscript, Minutes of Proposal Defense, IPMO similarity result. |
| TDW 10 | Pre-Final Evaluation Sheet | 2 | No scores. Comment boxes: Title and Preliminaries, Chapters 1 to 5, Partial References and Appendices, Remarks. General observations. Action Taken. |
| TDW 11 | Panel on Oral Defense Appointment and Acceptance Form | 3 | Adviser. Final Defense Panel: Chair plus 3 (thesis) or 4 (dissertation) members accept. Program Chair recommends. Dean/Director approves. |
| TDW 12 | Final Endorsement Form | 3 | Date of defense. Committee signs. Attachments: final manuscript, Minutes of Pre-Final Defense. Program Chair notes. Dean approves. |
| TDW 13 | Final Defense Evaluation Sheet | 3 | Same rubric as TDW 08. The header names the Final Defense Panel Chair and Members. Grade equivalent table. Action Taken. |

**BR-15:** TDW 08 and TDW 13 print a grade equivalent table. The system shows no grade and no grade equivalent.

**Note on terms:** The forms say "Evaluator", "Examiner" and "Final Defense Panel". The system uses Panel Member and Final Panel (`AGENTS.md` §8).

## Guidelines (summary)

| Phase | Steps | Main points |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| A. Concept Paper and Proposal | A.1 to A.5 | Concept paper of 3 to 4 pages, at least 1000 words. Dean appoints the Adviser. Advising contract. |
| B. Writing 1: Proposal | B.1 to B.20 | 18 units first. Chapters 1 to 3. Committee: Adviser plus 2 or 3 evaluators. NDA. Ethics review. 5 or 6 printed copies, at least one week before the defense. Certification of Proposal Approval. Data gathering with Adviser approval. |
| C. Writing 2: Pre-Final, Final, post-defense | C.1 to C.27 | Similarity check and IPMO before pre-defense. Pre-Final Defense and Minutes. Final Panel of 4 (thesis) or 5 (dissertation). New NDA. Final Defense and Minutes. Article, IPMO, RPO editing, signatures, copyright, final copy, conference, journal. |
