                  GUIDELINES ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MODIFIED
                           THESIS/DISSERTATION WRITING COURSES
                              (THESIS/DISSERTATION WRITING I AND II)

  Introduction

           The need to strengthen the research training and improve the quality of Student
  researchers produced in the different graduate programs has provided the impetus
  for the proposed modification of thesis/dissertation and dissertation writing courses
  in the University. This is also in response to CMO No 15, s. 2019 which states "at
  least one publication in an internationally/nationally indexed journal or juried creative
  work outlet" as one of the major requirements for granting graduate degrees by all
  higher education institutions. The proposal to split the 6-unit Thesis/Dissertation
  Writing course into two 3-unit courses (Thesis/Dissertation 1 and Thesis/Dissertation
  2) and the 12-unit Dissertation Writing into two 6- unit courses (Dissertation 1 and
  Dissertation 2) was approved by the Board of Regents during its 170th Regular
  Meeting on September 16, 2020.

  Guidelines for Implementation

  A. Phase 1: Concept Paper and Proposal Phase

       1. The Student is admitted in the program and submits to the Program
           Chairperson his/her concept paper, a tentative research proposal (3- to 4-page,
           1.5 spacing, at least 1000 words) defining his/her research interest and plan.

       2. The Program Chairperson and a member of the faculty evaluate the concept
           paper and, if found worthy of a graduate level research project, recommends
           a Thesis/Dissertation Adviser.

       3. The Dean/OU Director approves the recommendation, and formally informs
           the faculty of the request to serve as Adviser.

       4. If the faculty accepts the advising appointment, the Student enters into an
           Academic Advising Contract with the Adviser.

       5. The Student works closely with his/her Adviser in developing/revising and/or
           improving the concept paper and in establishing a clear plan for the research
           project.

  B. Phase 2: Thesis/Dissertation Writing 1: Proposal Writing

       1. Upon completion of at least eighteen (18) units of coursework (including
           Research Seminar and Statistics courses), the Student is advised to enroll
           RSH 641 Thesis Writing 1 or RSH 741 Dissertation Writing 1.

       2. The Student proceeds to write his/her research proposal based on the
           submitted concept paper.

       3. During the course, the Student is expected to engage in the following activities:
           - Collection, critical analysis, and evaluation of literature/ materials/ sources
           - Critical evaluation of accuracy and appropriateness of the research
              method(s) to be employed
           - Development of research instrument/s; Ascertaining the reliability and
              validity of research instrument/s and other techniques for inquiry.
           - Development and presentation of Chapters 1 to 3, complete with partial
              references and appendices.

       4. The Student submits the full thesis/dissertation proposal to the Program
           Chairperson. The Program Chair recommends the faculty evaluators to
           complete the Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation Committee.

       5. If the appointment is accepted by Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation Committee,
           proceed to next step. If not, Dean/OU Director assigns a new Thesis/
           Dissertation Evaluation Committee member.

       6. The Dean/OU Director officially assigns Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation
           Committee composed of the Adviser and the Evaluators (two evaluators for a
           thesis and three evaluators for a dissertation).

       7. Thesis/ Dissertation Evaluation Committee members sign a Confidentiality
           and Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) for the purpose of safeguarding
           confidential or propriety information, including data, technology, products,
           trade secrets, innovations, inventions that may be derived from the
           thesis/dissertation.

       8. The Thesis/Dissertation Adviser endorses, duly noted by the Program Chair,
           the thesis/dissertation proposal to the Graduate School Research Ethics
           Board (GSREB)/ College Research Ethics Board (CREB) / Open University
           Research Ethics Board (OUREB) for ethics review. If not approved, GSREB/
           CREB / OUREB asks the student to comply with the recommendations. The
           Research Ethics Approval Form shall be included in the appendices of
           thesis/dissertation manuscript.

       9. The Student submits five (5) copies of the thesis proposal or six (6) copies of
           dissertation proposal to the Program Chair. The Program Chair receives and
           endorses the proposal for defense. Copies of the proposal should be sent to
           the Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation Committee at least one week before the
           schedule of defense.

       18. Program Chair endorses for approval the survey questionnaire and

           correspondence to the Research Publications Office (RPO) for language
           editing.
       19. In case some parts of the manuscript (e.g., research instrument) need to be
           translated in Filipino language (or other dialect), the Student may seek the
           expert service from the Center for Translation Studies.
       20. The student, in close coordination with his/her Adviser, may secure permission
           of the concerned agency/office to gather data. With the approval of his/her
           Adviser, the Student may proceed with data-gathering, processing, analysis,
           and interpretation of results.

  C. Phase 3: Thesis/Dissertation Writing 2

       1. The Thesis/Dissertation Adviser, together with the Candidate, runs the revised
           manuscript for similarity or plagiarism check.

       2. The Candidate submits to the Intellectual Property Management Office (IPMO)
           the revised manuscript with the Adviser's written endorsement for final
           similarity or plagiarism check. If failed the test, resubmit for
           Similarity/Plagiarism Check.

       3. Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation Adviser endorses the complete manuscript for
           pre-defense.

       4. Program Chair receives and endorses to the Dean/OU Director the complete
           manuscript for pre-defense.

       5. GS/OU Secretary receives copies of Thesis/Dissertation Manuscript from the
           Candidate and schedules the Pre-Final Defense.

       6. Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation Committee scrutinizes the thesis/dissertation
           manuscript during the pre-final defense.

       7. The Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation Committee submits to the GS/OU
           Secretary the Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation Forms containing their
           respective comments/ recommendations.

       8. The GS/OU Secretary provides Student a Copy of Minutes of Pre-Final
           Defense containing consolidated recommendations which shall serve as
           guide in making the revisions.

       9. Thesis/Dissertation Adviser supervises the revision of thesis/dissertation
           manuscript.

       10. Thesis/Dissertation Evaluation Adviser endorses the full manuscript for final
           defense.

       11. Program Chair receives and endorses to the Dean/OU Director the full
           manuscript for final defense.

       12. Dean/OU Director assigns the Final Defense Panel, consisting of four (4)
           members for a thesis while five (5) members for a dissertation. The
           Thesis/Dissertation Evaluators are usually included in the Final Defense
           Panel.

       13. GS/OU Secretary distributes copies of thesis/dissertation manuscript to the
           Final Defense Panel and schedules the Final Defense.

       14. Members of the Final Defense Panel sign a Confidentiality and Non-
           Disclosure Agreement for the purpose of safeguarding confidential or
           propriety information.

       15. Final Defense Panel scrutinizes the manuscript and gives their comments/
           recommendations.

       16. The Panel submits to the GS/OU Secretary the Final Defense Rating Sheet
           indicating the grade (if the final defense was successful).

       17. The GS/OU Secretary provides the Candidate with Minutes of Final Defense
           which shall serve as guide in revising the thesis/ dissertation.

       18. Thesis/Dissertation Adviser collaborates with the Candidate in writing a
           publishable article based on the thesis/dissertation.

       19. The Thesis/Dissertation Adviser, together with the Candidate, runs the revised
           manuscript for similarity or plagiarism check.

       20. The Candidate submits to the Intellectual Property Management Office (IPMO)
           the revised manuscript with the Adviser's written endorsement for final
           similarity or plagiarism check.

       21. The Program Chairperson endorses the manuscript to the Research
           Publication Office for full manuscript editing.

       22. The Thesis/Dissertation Adviser supervises the revision of the final manuscript
           based on RPO editing.
