1.3 Statement of the Problem

Subterranean termites cause structural damage to wooden houses in many barangays before any sign is visible. Manual inspection is slow and depends on the skill of the inspector.

This study aims to detect termite activity early using acoustic emission. Specifically, it seeks to answer the following questions:

1. What acoustic features separate termite feeding from background noise in timber?

2. How accurate is a low-cost sensor node compared with a commercial detector?

1.4 Scope and Delimitation

The study covers three species common in Metro Manila and tests the node on pine and narra samples under laboratory conditions.
