# C6 · Defense

**Route:** `/coordinator/defense/[defenseId]` · **Role:** Coordinator · **Density:** compact · **FRs:** FR-DEF-08 to FR-DEF-10, FR-WFL-06, FR-REV-13

## Job
Verify the evaluation sheets, issue the Minutes, and close the stage.

## Regions
Uses the C4 inbox (`C4.md`) for the sheets.

| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Identity line | Identity line | Thesis title, Student, milestone, defense date |
| 2 | Entry tracker | Queue table | Each member: sheet saved or not, scan uploaded, verified |
| 3 | Sheet check | Inbox (list, scan, record) | Each sheet scan with Verify (matched) and Return with a reason (mismatch, the sheet reopens), as on C4 |
| 4 | Verdict | Verdict panel, read only | Members' totals and actions taken, the verdict, any flags |
| 5 | Minutes and revisions | Ruled section | Issue the Minutes. The revision items. |
| 6 | Close | Docket action | "Close stage" (maroon). After close: Certification. |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| Not ready to close | "Close stage" is disabled: "A sheet is not verified." or the Minutes reason (SITEMAP §9) |
| Closed | `done` pill. The manuscript unlocks (BR-07). |

## Rules
- Close needs every sheet verified and the Minutes issued.
- Only the Coordinator closes a stage (BR-02).
