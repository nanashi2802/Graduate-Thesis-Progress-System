# P4 · Defense Day and verdict

**Route:** `/panel/defense/[defenseId]` · **Role:** Panel Member · **Density:** Defense Day (tablet, 48 px targets) · **FRs:** FR-DEF-05 to FR-DEF-07, FR-REV-12

## Job
Let each member encode their sheet, and let the Lead Panel Member or Panel Chair record the verdict.

## Regions
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Header | Identity line | Thesis title, Student, milestone, date. A substitute banner if the member is a substitute. |
| 2 | Tabs | Tabs | My evaluation, Manuscript and comments (Final only), Verdict |
| 3 | My evaluation | Defense Day form | The form for the milestone (FR-DEF-05), row for row: indicator code (mono), name, weight, a 48 px number stepper. The automatic total. A comments box. "Save my evaluation". |
| 4 | Verdict | Verdict panel | Every member's total and action taken, then the verdict control |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| Before the start | "Opens at the scheduled start." (BR-10) |
| Saved | "Saved. Sign the paper sheet." (BR-01) |
| Verdict tab, before the date | The verdict control is disabled: "Allowed on or after the defense date." |
| Verdict tab, open | Loads the saved sheets when it opens. A Refresh button with "Updated hh:mm". It refreshes every 30 s while open. |
| Missing sheet | A flag: "1 sheet is not saved yet." It never blocks (F2). |
| Action Taken differs | A flag on that member's row (FR-DEF-07, BR-17) |

## Rules
- Evaluations are not live. Members save sheets (FR-DEF-06).
- The verdict is recorded on or after the defense date (UTP owner decision R6).
- The Verdict tab shows only to the Lead Panel Member (Proposal, Pre-Final) or the Panel Chair (Final).
- Numbers only. No grade, no grade equivalent, no color on scores (F4).
- Works on a tablet in portrait and landscape (NFR-09).
