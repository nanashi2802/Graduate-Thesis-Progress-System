# A4 · Availability

**Route:** `/adviser/availability` · **Role:** Adviser · **Density:** comfortable · **FRs:** FR-SCH-01, FR-SCH-02, FR-SCH-07

## Job
Answer consultation requests and publish open slots.

## Regions (in this order)
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Requests | Queue table | Each request: Student, slot, agenda. Actions: Confirm, Decline (same width). |
| 2 | My slots | Slot list | Published slots: when, length, kind (Consultation or Defense), place or link |
| 3 | Publish a slot | Side-sheet form | Kind (Consultation or Defense), In person or Online, room or meeting link, date and time, repeat |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| First day | Requests: "No requests. Advisees request a consultation from your open slots." Slots: "No slots yet. Publish a slot so advisees can book." "Publish a slot" is the one maroon action. |

## Rules
- Slots can repeat.
- The meeting link shows only to the Adviser and the booked Student (FR-SCH-03).
