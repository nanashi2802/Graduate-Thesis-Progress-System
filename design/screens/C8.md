# C8 · Templates

**Route:** `/coordinator/templates` · **Role:** Coordinator · **Density:** compact · **FRs:** FR-TPL-01 to FR-TPL-05, FR-NTF-10

## Job
Keep the milestone templates, the parameters, the forms and the email wording.

## Regions
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Tabs | Tabs | Templates, Forms, Parameters, Emails |
| 2 | Templates | Queue table + side sheet | Each template: name, version, in use by (count). The side sheet edits deliverables, signers, sizes by degree level, printed-copy counts, rubric indicators and weights, target dates. |
| 3 | Forms | Queue table | The 13 TDW forms: form number, name, file, "Replace". Defaults come from `docs/reference/tdw-forms`. |
| 4 | Parameters | Form | Including "Advisee limit: Off / number", default Off (FR-TRK-06) |
| 5 | Emails | Queue table + side sheet | Each email template and its wording (FR-NTF-10) |

## Rules
- A change makes a new template version. Running instances keep their version (FR-TPL-04).
- A rubric whose weights do not total 100% is rejected. The save shows the reason (FR-TPL-03).
- The advisee limit is Off by default. Its number is `[CONFIRM]`.
