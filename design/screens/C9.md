# C9 · Users

**Route:** `/coordinator/users` · **Role:** Coordinator · **Density:** compact · **FRs:** FR-ACC-05, FR-ACC-06

## Job
Add faculty accounts and keep each user's role and email.

## Regions
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | User list | Queue table | Name, role, email, status, Action ("Edit"). Filter chips by role. |
| 2 | Add or edit | Side-sheet form | Name, email, role (Adviser or Panel Member for faculty). "Add user". |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| First day | "No faculty yet. Add Advisers and Panel Members before you create Student accounts." "Add faculty" is the one maroon action. |

## Rules
- One role per account (BR-03). The role field explains this in the side sheet only.
