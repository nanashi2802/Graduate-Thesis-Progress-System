# S4 · Schedule

**Route:** `/student/schedule` · **Role:** Student · **Density:** comfortable · **FRs:** FR-SCH-02 to FR-SCH-04, FR-SCH-08, FR-DEF-01

## Job
Book consultations with the Adviser and propose the defense date.

## Regions
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Tabs | Tabs | Consultations, Defense date |
| 2 | Next booking (Consultations) | Card (1 of 3) | The next consultation: date, place or meeting link, agenda |
| 3 | Open slots | Slot list | The Adviser's open slots. 3 facts for each row: when, length, place. Selecting a row fills the request form. |
| 4 | Request form | Form | Agenda, linked threads, "Request consultation" |
| 5 | Defense date tab | Date picker with limits | Locked before Gate 1b. After it, a calendar. Dates under 7 days after Gate 1b are greyed out with the reason (BR-09). "Propose date" and a direct link to TDW 07. |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| First day | Next booking: "No consultation booked. Pick one of your Adviser's open slots." If the Adviser has no slots: "Your Adviser has no open slots yet." |
| Defense date locked | "Locked until Gate 1b." and the steps left before it |
| Date proposed | The proposed date with a `pending` pill, until the Coordinator confirms it |

## Rules
- The meeting link shows only to the Adviser and the booked Student (FR-SCH-03).
