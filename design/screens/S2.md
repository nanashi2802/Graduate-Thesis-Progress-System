# S2 · Manuscript workspace

**Route:** `/student/pr/[prId]` · **Role:** Student · **Density:** comfortable · **FRs:** FR-REV-01 to FR-REV-08, FR-REV-14

## Job
Upload manuscript versions and answer the reviewers' comments.

## Regions
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Identity line | Identity line | Thesis title, degree level, milestone |
| 2 | Merge box | Merge box (Primer) | One sentence that says what to do now, and one action ("Mark Ready" or "Upload version"). When locked, the action becomes its reason. |
| 3 | Commit selector | Commit selector | Version, short hash, date, uploader. The Ready commit has an `active` tag. |
| 4 | Workspace | Workspace: PDF viewer + thread feed | The PDF on the left. The thread feed on the right (400 px), tabs Open (first), All, Resolved. |
| 5 | Upload bar | Sticky bottom bar | Upload a new version, change note, "Addressed" tags. "More" holds the comment-response export. |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| First day | No commit. The viewer area is an empty state: "Upload your first version. Your Adviser sees it after you mark it Ready." |
| Ready | The Ready commit has its tag. The merge box says who reviews next. |
| Locked (after Gate 1b) | The lock banner across the viewer (BR-07). The upload bar is replaced by the reason: "Locked until the defense is closed." |

## Rules
- The Student replies but cannot resolve (BR-05).
- An upload clears Ready and marks older open threads Outdated.
- A thread may carry a page number: "p. 14" moves the viewer to that page (FR-REV-04). No highlights or pins on the PDF (ADR-009).
- No edit or delete control on a commit (BR-19).
