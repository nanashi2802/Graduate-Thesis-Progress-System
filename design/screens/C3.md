# C3 · Committees

**Route:** `/coordinator/committees` · **Role:** Coordinator · **Density:** compact · **FRs:** FR-CMT-01 to FR-CMT-06

## Job
Appoint the Evaluation Committee and the Final Panel, and record the paper.

## Regions
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Appointment board | Queue table | One row for each Student who needs a committee: Student, milestone, seats filled, paper state, Action ("Appoint") |
| 2 | Appoint | Side-sheet form | Seats at the size for the degree level. Each seat: faculty member (with workload and availability, FR-CMT-02), status (Proposed, Accepted, Declined). Lead Panel Member and Panel Chair designations. TDW 06 or TDW 11 scan, NDA scans. "Mark appointed". |
| 3 | Substitute | Side-sheet form | Record a substitute from the existing Panel Member accounts, with their NDA (FR-CMT-06) |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| Declined seat | The seat shows `returned` and "Needs a replacement" |
| First day | "No committees to appoint. Students appear here after Gate 1a." |

## Rules
- Panel Members are appointed after Gate 1a in Milestones 1 and 3 (FR-CMT-01).
- The Adviser pool and the Panel Member pool are separate.
- Manuscript access opens when the form and the member's NDA are both recorded (BR-08).
- The advisee-limit flag shows here only if the C8 parameter is on (FR-TRK-06).
