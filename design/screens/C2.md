# C2 · Students

**Route:** `/coordinator/students` · **Role:** Coordinator · **Density:** compact · **FRs:** FR-ACC-04, FR-ACC-07

## Job
Create Student accounts after eligibility, and keep their eligibility data.

## Regions
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Student list | Queue table | Columns: Student, Degree level, Adviser, Milestone, Status, Action ("Open"). Filter chips. |
| 2 | Create account | Side-sheet form | Name, email, degree level, Adviser, working thesis title (FR-ACC-04), eligibility data. "Create account". |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| First day | "No Students yet. Create a Student account after the Student is eligible." "Add a Student" is the one maroon action. |

## Rules
- Create an account only after eligibility. The system creates the Thesis Instance and Milestone 1.
- Residency shows as a flag glyph with its reason in a tooltip (BR-17).
- The Student can edit the title until Gate 1b (FR-ACC-09).
