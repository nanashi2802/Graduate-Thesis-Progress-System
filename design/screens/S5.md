# S5 · Defense results

**Route:** `/student/defense/[defenseId]` · **Role:** Student · **Density:** comfortable · **FRs:** FR-DEF-11, FR-DEF-12

## Job
Show the defense result after the Coordinator verifies the sheets.

## Regions
| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Identity line | Identity line | Thesis title, milestone, defense date |
| 2 | Verdict | Card (1 of 3) | The verdict (the action taken) in words |
| 3 | Member results | Table | Each member's total as a number, and their action taken |
| 4 | Minutes | Ruled section | The Minutes document, once issued |
| 5 | Next steps | Task list | The revision items and the next step |
| 6 | Attempt history | Collapsed group | Earlier attempts at this defense, if any |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| Before verification | The sidebar item is locked: "Opens after the Coordinator verifies the sheets." (BR-11) |
| Verified | As above |

## Rules
- No grade and no grade equivalent (BR-15, F4). Totals are numbers in ink.
