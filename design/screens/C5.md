# C5 · Schedule

**Route:** `/coordinator/schedule` · **Role:** Coordinator · **Density:** compact · **FRs:** FR-DEF-03, FR-DEF-04, FR-DOC-08

## Job
Confirm the proposed defense dates and rooms, and track the printed copies.

## Regions
Uses the C4 inbox (`C4.md`).

| # | Region | Pattern | Content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | List | Inbox list | Date requests, oldest first: Student, proposed date, milestone, age |
| 2 | Center | Ruled sections | The proposed date, the members' availability for it, the date checklist (FR-DOC-08), the printed copies due 7 days before (BR-09) |
| 3 | Record | Inbox record | Room or link field. Two full-width actions: "Confirm date" (maroon) and Return (Return composer). |

## States
| State | What shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| Checklist incomplete | "Confirm date" is disabled. The reason names the missing item (SITEMAP §9). |
| Late printed copies | A flag (BR-17). It never blocks. |
| First day | "No date requests. Requests appear here after a Student proposes a date." |

## Rules
- The defense date is at least 7 days after Gate 1b (BR-09).
