EchoWood: Embedded Termite Detection via ESP32-S3 Acoustic Emission Modeling

A Master's Thesis Submitted by Juan Dela Cruz

3.2 Engineering Methodology & Acoustic Modeling

The experimental setup employs an ESP32-S3 microcontroller running FreeRTOS dual-core firmware. Piezoelectric acoustic contact transducers (Murata 7BB-20-6L0) are bonded to timber test specimens using cyanoacrylate coupling gel. Acoustic emission signals generated by worker termites during structural mandible shearing exhibit spectral peaks between 12 kHz and 48 kHz.
