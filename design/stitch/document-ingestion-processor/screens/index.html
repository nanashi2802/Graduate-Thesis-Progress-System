Architecture
3 Milestones + Clearance

Proposal (M1), Pre-Final (M2), Final & Clearance (M3). Stage 0 outside.

Strict Segregation
1 Role Per Account

Rule BR-03: Advisers are never Panel Members; Coordinator is neither.

Sitemap Coverage
30 Official Routes

Partitioned across Student, Adviser, Panel Member, and Coordinator.

Compliance Standards
ASD-STE100 & 107 FRs

Two-Layer Approval: Scans provisional until physical paper verified.

Student Portal (Screens S1 - S6 & SV)

— Candidate workspace for submission, scheduling, and tracking
S1 /student/dashboard
Thesis Tracker & Stepper

Identity card, 3-milestone progress stepper, next-action card, readiness checklist, outside-status chips, key dates.
S2 /student/pr/104
Manuscript Review Workspace

Commit selector (v1, v2), PDF reader mock, critique threads feed (All/Open/Resolved), change note authoring, export responses.
S3 /student/documents
Deliverables & Form Uploads

Official deliverables checklist (TDW 03, 05, 06, 07), prefilled PDF generator, ZIP form pack bundle, provisional scan upload.
S4 /student/schedule
Consultations & Defense Scheduler

Book adviser slots with protected online meeting links, choose defense date from common free dates (minimum 7-day rule).
S5 /student/defense/[id]
Defense Results & Minutes

Visible after Coordinator verification. Collective verdict banner, member weighted rubric scores (no grade equivalents), Minutes download.
S6 /student/clearance
Post-Defense Clearance

7-item clearance tracker: IPMO, RPO, Gate 2, copyright, hardbound copies, conference presentation cert, journal publication.

Adviser Portal (Screens A1 - A5 & SV)

— Faculty adviser workspace for reviewing commits, gates, and consultations
A1 /adviser/dashboard
Advisee Roster & Action Queue

Prioritized action queue with aging in days, advisee table with pacing flags (days in milestone, days since commit).
A2 /adviser/pr/104
Manuscript Review & Gate 1a

Split screen review, Gate 1a endorsement button with open thread blocker tooltip, Return with written reason modal.
A3 /adviser/consultations
TDW Form 03 Consultation Log

Official consultation log builder, student agenda, adviser recommendations, upload signed scan, export PDF.
A4 /adviser/availability
Availability & Slots Publisher

Publish in-person and online consultation hours, meeting link protection, pending student requests confirmation.
A5 /adviser/defense/[id]
Defense Day Evaluation Sheet

Encode TDW 08 (Proposal) or TDW 10 (Pre-Final) evaluation criteria. Notice: Adviser completes no TDW 13 at Final.

Panel Member Portal (Screens P1 - P4 & SV)

— Committee examination workspace for reviewing locked manuscripts and encoding verdicts
P1 /panel/dashboard
Assigned Committees & Queue

Overview of assigned Master's and Doctoral defense panels. Access locked until TDW 06/11 and NDA recorded.
P2 /panel/pr/104
Manuscript Reader & Gate 1b

Review locked manuscript, resolve committee critique threads, endorse Gate 1b to lock manuscript for scheduling.
P3 /panel/availability
Defense Availability Publisher

Set available days and times to calculate common free dates for candidate defense proposals.
P4 /panel/defense/[id]
Defense Day & Collective Verdict

Encode individual rubric sheets (TDW 08 / 13). Lead Panel Member / Panel Chair records collective verdict.

Coordinator Portal (Screens C1 - C10 & SV)

— Program Chair and Secretary management hub (Only role that closes stages)
C1 /coordinator/dashboard
Program Overview & KPIs

Master KPI strip, candidate roster, aging reports, CSV export triggers.
C2 /coordinator/students
Student Accounts & Eligibility

Register eligible students after Stage 0 concept paper & enrollment confirmation.
C3 /coordinator/committees
Committee Appointments & NDAs

Late binding committee appointments after Gate 1a, upload TDW 06/11 and NDAs.
C4 /coordinator/documents
Document Compliance & Verification

Verify wet-signed paper scans against digital files, flag mismatches (BR-12).
C5 /coordinator/schedule
Schedule & Printed Copies

Confirm defense venue and audit physical printed copies match against locked SHA-256 hash.
C6 /coordinator/defense/[id]
Defense Verification & Minutes

Verify signed rubric sheets, upload official Minutes, close defense stage to unlock manuscript.
C7 /coordinator/clearance
Clearance & Outside Statuses

Control Ethics, IPMO, RPO, and Copyright statuses, verify evidence, grant CLEARED.
C8 /coordinator/templates
Milestone Templates & Parameters

Configure required TDW forms, 7-day defense notice rules, and rubric weights (total = 100%).
C9 /coordinator/users
Faculty & User Registry

Enforce strict 1-role-per-account compliance (BR-03). Advisers cannot be Panel Members.
C10 /coordinator/audit
System Audit Trail & Log

Immutable, append-only log of gate actions, returns, uploads, and transactional email events.
