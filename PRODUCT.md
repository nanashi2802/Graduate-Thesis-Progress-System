# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Students** (MSCpE and D.Eng with CpE specialization) who write a graduate thesis. They want to know where they stand and what to do next.
- **Advisers** who guide several students at once and review each manuscript version.
- **Panel Members** who read, comment on and evaluate a thesis at its defenses.
- **The Coordinator** who runs the program: accounts, panels, schedules and stage closes.
- All visitors to the landing page already have an account. The Coordinator creates accounts; there is no self sign-up.
- Presentation audience (confirmed 23 Nov 2026): faculty and Graduate School users who are about to adopt the system. They must leave wanting to sign in.

## Product Purpose

ThesIsko keeps a graduate thesis on one shared track from the Proposal Defense through the Pre-Final and Final Defense to clearance. Everyone involved sees the same status, the same manuscript version and the next action. Success: no one asks "where is my thesis?" or "which version did you comment on?" again.

## Positioning

ThesIsko treats a thesis like versioned work. Each manuscript upload is a version, and feedback is pinned to the version it was written on, so comments survive revisions. A general LMS or a shared drive cannot say that truthfully.

## Operating Context

- A thesis moves through three defenses and a clearance step. Consultations with the Adviser happen between them.
- Signed paper forms remain the official record. The system tracks and coordinates around them.
- Home institution: PUP Graduate School, Department of Computer Engineering.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Thesis tracker with one next action per student; version history with pinned review threads; consultation booking and log; defense scheduling; panel evaluation on defense day; clearance checklist; notifications; Coordinator overview.
- One role per account (Student, Adviser, Panel Member, Coordinator).
- Not a grading tool, not an e-signature tool.
- Free-tier hosting only.

## Product Principles

1. One next action: every person always knows what to do now.
2. Feedback belongs to a version.
3. One shared truth across all four roles.
4. Calm over clever: the system reduces anxiety around a stressful process.
